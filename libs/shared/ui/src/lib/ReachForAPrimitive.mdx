<h1 className='doc-title'>Reach for a Primitive</h1>
<p className='doc-lead'>
  A one-page cheat-sheet: before you type a raw HTML element, check whether a
  primitive already owns it. Nearly every interactive element here ships focus
  states, dark mode, and accessibility you'd otherwise re-implement (usually
  incompletely). When one almost fits, extend it — see{' '}
  <a href='?path=/docs/guides-extend-don-t-reimplement--docs'>
    Extend, Don't Reimplement
  </a>
  .
</p>

<div className='doc-note'>
  <strong>How to read this.</strong> "Instead of" is the raw thing people reach
  for out of habit. "Reach for" is the primitive that already solves it. "You'd
  otherwise re-derive" is what you silently take on by hand-rolling — almost
  always accessibility.
</div>
